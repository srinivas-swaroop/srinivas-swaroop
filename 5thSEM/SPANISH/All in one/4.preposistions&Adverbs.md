# 🔵 **1. SOBRE — on / over / above**

**Use when something is ON TOP OF a surface (touching or not touching).**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **sobre** la caja.
**English:**
The apple is **on / over** the box.

✔ Touching = OK
✔ Floating above = OK

---

# 🔵 **2. AL LADO DE — next to / beside**

**Use when something is very close, on one side.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **al lado de** la caja.
**English:**
The apple is **next to** the box.

✔ Not touching
✔ Just nearby

---

# 🔵 **3. ENCIMA DE — on top of**

**Use when something is directly on top (touching) another object.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **encima de** la caja.
**English:**
The apple is **on top of** the box.

✔ Must be touching

---

# 🔵 **4. DEBAJO DE — under / below**

**Use when something is under another object.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **debajo de** la caja.
**English:**
The apple is **under** the box.

✔ Opposite of “encima de”

---

# 🔵 **5. DELANTE DE — in front of**

**Use when something is in front (before) another object.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **delante de** la caja.
**English:**
The apple is **in front of** the box.

---

# 🔵 **6. DETRÁS DE — behind**

**Use when something is behind another object.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **detrás de** la caja.
**English:**
The apple is **behind** the box.

✔ Opposite of “delante de”

---

# 🔵 **7. EN MEDIO DE — in the middle of**

**Use when something is in the center of more than one object.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **en medio de** las cajas.
**English:**
The apple is **in the middle of** the boxes.

✔ Surrounded on two or more sides

---

# 🔵 **8. ENTRE — between**

**Use when something is between exactly two objects.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **entre** dos cajas.
**English:**
The apple is **between** two boxes.

✔ Only TWO sides

---

# 🔵 **9. DENTRO DE — inside**

**Use when something is inside / within something else.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **dentro de** la caja.
**English:**
The apple is **inside** the box.

✔ Opposite: fuera de

---

# 🔵 **10. FUERA DE — outside**

**Use when something is not inside.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **fuera de** la caja.
**English:**
The apple is **outside** the box.

---

# 🔵 **11. A LA IZQUIERDA DE — to the left of**

**Use when something is on the left side.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **a la izquierda de** la caja.
**English:**
The apple is **to the left of** the box.

---

# 🔵 **12. A LA DERECHA DE — to the right of**

**Use when something is on the right side.**

**Spanish:**
La manzana está **a la derecha de** la caja.
**English:**
The apple is **to the right of** the box.

# ⭐ **ADVERBIOS DE LUGAR (Place Adverbs)**

These words tell you **WHERE** something is.

We have three important ones in Spanish:

1. **aquí**
2. **ahí**
3. **allí**

Let’s explain clearly.

---

# 🔵 **1. AQUÍ — “here”**

📌 **Used when the object or person is VERY CLOSE to the speaker.**
(near ME)

Distance: **0–1 meters** (touching or close)

**Spanish:**
¿Vives tú **aquí**?
Sí, vivo **aquí**.

**English:**
Do you live **here**?
Yes, I live **here**.

**Examples:**

* Estoy **aquí**. → I am here.
* Deja el libro **aquí**. → Leave the book here.

**TRICK:**
👉 Use **aquí** when the thing is **near you**.

---

# 🟠 **2. AHÍ — “there”**

📌 **Used when something is at a medium distance**
(not too close, not too far)

Distance: **1–5 meters**

**Spanish:**
Hay unos edificios nuevos **ahí**.
**English:**
There are some new buildings **there**.

**Examples:**

* Pon la mochila **ahí**. → Put the backpack there.
* Tu teléfono está **ahí**, en la mesa. → Your phone is there, on the table.

**TRICK:**
👉 **ahí** = “there beside you / in that area” (medium distance)

---

# 🔴 **3. ALLÍ — “over there”**

📌 **Used when something is FAR from both speaker and listener.**

Distance: **far away, maybe across the room or park**

**Spanish:**
Los niños están **allí** en el parque.
**English:**
The children are **over there** in the park.

**Examples:**

* Mira **allí**. → Look over there!
* El restaurante está **allí**, al final de la calle. → The restaurant is over there, at the end of the street.

**TRICK:**
👉 Use **allí** for **pointing to something far**.

---

# ⭐ COMPARISON TABLE (Simple & Clear)

| Spanish  | English    | Distance        |
| -------- | ---------- | --------------- |
| **aquí** | here       | very close      |
| **ahí**  | there      | medium distance |
| **allí** | over there | far away        |

---

# ⭐ SUPER EASY MEMORY TRICK

Think like this:

### 🔵 **AQUÍ = A + QUI**

“**A-QUI**ckly close to me.”

### 🟠 **AHÍ = A-HI**

“**A-hi**, not too far, not too close.”

### 🔴 **ALLÍ = A-LLI**

Say the **LL** like “y”, long sound → feels *far*.

---

# ⭐ More Useful Examples (Sentence Practice)

**AQUÍ (near me):**

* Yo estoy **aquí**.
  → I am here.

**AHÍ (near you/medium):**

* Tu mochila está **ahí**, en la silla.
  → Your backpack is there, on the chair.

**ALLÍ (far):**

* Mi casa está **allí**, en aquella calle.
  → My house is over there, on that street.
