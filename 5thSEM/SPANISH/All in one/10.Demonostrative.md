# 🟦 **ARTÍCULOS Y DEMOSTRATIVOS (Simple Explanation)**

Below is the list you must use:

### **[EL, UNAS, ESA, ESTOS, ESTAS, ESAS, ESTE, AQUELLOS]**

---

# ✅ **1. ARTICLES (ARTÍCULOS)**

### **EL**

➡️ Meaning: **the** (masculine singular)
➡️ Used for: one male object
**Example:** el libro = the book

### **UNAS**

➡️ Meaning: **some** (feminine plural)
➡️ Used for: many female objects
**Example:** unas casas = some houses

---

# ✅ **2. DEMONSTRATIVES (DEMONSTRATIVOS)**

Think like pointing with your finger.

### **ESTE / ESTOS / ESTAS** → **NEAR** (close to you)

#### **ESTE**

➡️ Meaning: **this** (masculine singular)
➡️ Near me
**Example:** este coche = this car (near me)

#### **ESTOS**

➡️ Meaning: **these** (masculine plural)
➡️ Near me
**Example:** estos libros = these books

#### **ESTAS**

➡️ Meaning: **these** (feminine plural)
➡️ Near me
**Example:** estas casas = these houses

---

### **ESA / ESAS** → **MIDDLE** (close to the listener)

#### **ESA**

➡️ Meaning: **that** (feminine singular)
➡️ Near you (not too far)
**Example:** esa mesa = that table

#### **ESAS**

➡️ Meaning: **those** (feminine plural)
➡️ Plural feminine
**Example:** esas chicas = those girls

---

### **AQUELLOS** → **FAR AWAY**

#### **AQUELLOS**

➡️ Meaning: **those (far away)** (masculine plural)
➡️ Very far
**Example:** aquellos edificios = those buildings (far away)

---

# 🟢 HOW TO IDENTIFY WHICH ONE TO USE?

Look for **3 things**:

### 1️⃣ **Gender** (masculine/feminine)

* Masculine → el, este, estos, aquel, aquellos
* Feminine → esa, esas, estas, unas

### 2️⃣ **Number** (singular/plural)

* Singular → el, este, esa
* Plural → unos/unas, estos, estas, esas, aquellos

### 3️⃣ **Distance**

* Near me → este, estos, estas
* Near you → esa, esas
* Far away → aquellos
* Neutral → el, unas (not about distance)

# ✅ **COMPARATIVOS (Comparatives)**

**To compare two things in Spanish.**

---

# ⭐ 1. **TAN… COMO**

### ✔ Use this for **equality** → “as… as”

### **Spanish meaning:**

Se usa para comparar dos cosas que son iguales.

### **English meaning:**

Used to compare two things that are equal.

### **Example:**

* *Este libro es **tan** bueno **como** ese.*
  → This book is **as** good **as** that one.

---

# ⭐ 2. **MÁS… QUE**

### ✔ Use this for **more than**

### **Example:**

* *Mi casa es **más** grande **que** la tuya.*
  → My house is **bigger** than yours.

---

# ⭐ 3. **MENOS… QUE**

### ✔ Use this for **less than**

### **Example:**

* *Ella es **menos** alta **que** su hermano.*
  → She is **less** tall than her brother.

---

# ⭐ 4. **MEJOR / PEOR**

These are **irregular comparatives**.
You **do NOT** use “más” before them.

### **MEJOR = better**

### **PEOR = worse**

### **Spanish explanation:**

Son comparativos especiales. No necesitan “más”.

### **English explanation:**

These are special comparatives. They don’t need “more”.

### **Examples:**

* *Este restaurante es **mejor** que ese.*
  → This restaurant is **better** than that one.

* *Este restaurante es **peor** que ese.*
  → This restaurant is **worse** than that one.

# ❓ **Your question:**

### *Why can’t we use “tan” in the sentence?*

The question in the exam is:

**Este restaurante es ___ (tan, como, peor) que ese.**

You must choose **ONE word** from the bracket.

Notice:

* **tan** is NOT complete alone
  You must use **tan + adjective + como**
  Example: **tan bueno como**
* The exam does NOT give you *bueno* or *como*
* So **“tan” alone is grammatically wrong**
* **como** alone also makes no sense
* Only **peor** fits because it is a complete comparative word by itself

✔ Correct:
**Este restaurante es peor que ese.**
This restaurant is worse than that one.

❌ Wrong:
**Este restaurante es tan que ese.** (incorrect grammar)
**Este restaurante es como que ese.** (incorrect grammar)

---

# 🧠 **Easy Trick to Remember**

* **tan** ALWAYS needs **como** and an **adjective**
  📌 *“tan + adjective + como” → “as … as”*
* **peor** and **mejor** are independent words
  📌 *“peor que” → worse than*
  📌 *“mejor que” → better than*
