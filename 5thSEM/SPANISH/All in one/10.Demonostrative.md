# 🟦 **ARTÍCULOS Y DEMOSTRATIVOS (Simple Explanation)**

Below is the list you must use:

### **[EL, UNAS, ESA, ESTOS, ESTAS, ESAS, ESTE, AQUELLOS]**

---

# ✅ **1. ARTICLES (ARTÍCULOS)**

### **EL**

➡️ Meaning: **the** (masculine singular)
➡️ Used for: one male object
**Example:** el libro = the book

### **UNAS**

➡️ Meaning: **some** (feminine plural)
➡️ Used for: many female objects
**Example:** unas casas = some houses

---

# ✅ **2. DEMONSTRATIVES (DEMONSTRATIVOS)**

Think like pointing with your finger.

### **ESTE / ESTOS / ESTAS** → **NEAR** (close to you)

#### **ESTE**

➡️ Meaning: **this** (masculine singular)
➡️ Near me
**Example:** este coche = this car (near me)

#### **ESTOS**

➡️ Meaning: **these** (masculine plural)
➡️ Near me
**Example:** estos libros = these books

#### **ESTAS**

➡️ Meaning: **these** (feminine plural)
➡️ Near me
**Example:** estas casas = these houses

---

### **ESA / ESAS** → **MIDDLE** (close to the listener)

#### **ESA**

➡️ Meaning: **that** (feminine singular)
➡️ Near you (not too far)
**Example:** esa mesa = that table

#### **ESAS**

➡️ Meaning: **those** (feminine plural)
➡️ Plural feminine
**Example:** esas chicas = those girls

---

### **AQUELLOS** → **FAR AWAY**

#### **AQUELLOS**

➡️ Meaning: **those (far away)** (masculine plural)
➡️ Very far
**Example:** aquellos edificios = those buildings (far away)

---

# 🟢 HOW TO IDENTIFY WHICH ONE TO USE?

Look for **3 things**:

### 1️⃣ **Gender** (masculine/feminine)

* Masculine → el, este, estos, aquel, aquellos
* Feminine → esa, esas, estas, unas

### 2️⃣ **Number** (singular/plural)

* Singular → el, este, esa
* Plural → unos/unas, estos, estas, esas, aquellos

### 3️⃣ **Distance**

* Near me → este, estos, estas
* Near you → esa, esas
* Far away → aquellos
* Neutral → el, unas (not about distance)
